How it works

  1. 1

    Find your school

    Search by name, URN or DfE number below. Your school's details are filled in for you.

  2. 2

    Work through the standards

    Rate each standard, note what's in place and who owns it. A built-in guide shows you what evidence to gather.

  3. 3

    Plan and share

    Set dates for what you're working towards, export to Excel and print a report for your governors.

Get started

Find your school

Look up your school once and its details will be filled in for you in the trackers.

For example 100000, 352/4000 or 3524000

    Find your trust

    For multi-academy trusts. Each school fills in its trackers and selects Export Excel, then sends the files to the trust. The trust imports them all on the Trust Overview page to see every academy together.

    For example "Aspire Community Trust" or 16748

      DfE Digital & Technology Standards

      Rate your school or college against the Department for Education's digital and technology standards, including the six core standards.

      • 12 standard areas, RAG rated
      • Notes, recommendations and ownership
      • School details filled in from your school search
      • Print-ready report for governors
      Open DfE tracker →

      Trust Overview

      For multi-academy trusts: bring together the tracker exports from each of your schools and see the whole trust at a glance.

      • Every academy against every standard area
      • Core standards, filtering and Cyber Essentials readiness
      • Outstanding actions and a trust Excel export
      Open trust overview →

      Cyber Essentials

      Check readiness for Cyber Essentials certification against the NCSC's current requirements, with notes on how each applies in schools.

      • Scope plus the five technical controls
      • Evidence, actions and owners
      • Certification and renewal dates
      • Print-ready report for governors
      Open Cyber Essentials tracker →

      Software Register

      Keep a record of every piece of software and cloud service your school uses, with the details the DfE cyber security standard and Cyber Essentials ask for.

      • Licensing, support and end-of-life dates
      • Updates, MFA and data protection checks
      • Owners, renewals and an Excel export
      Open software register →

      Hardware and Asset Register

      Record every device and piece of network equipment, with the details the DfE standards and Cyber Essentials ask for.

      • Devices, network kit and where they are
      • Operating system support, warranty and replacement dates
      • Secure disposal and an Excel export
      Open hardware register →

      User Accounts and Access Privileges Register

      Keep track of who has access to what, especially administrator and third-party accounts, and when access was last reviewed.

      • Admin, staff, service and supplier accounts
      • MFA, separate admin accounts and leavers
      • Termly access reviews and an Excel export
      Open accounts register →

      Backup and Disaster Recovery Register

      Record what is backed up, where and how often, and log restore tests, so you know you can recover after an incident.

      • Backup coverage, including offline and cloud copies
      • Restore test log and recovery times
      • Disaster recovery contacts and an Excel export
      Open backup register →

      Cyber Incident and Breach Log

      Log cyber incidents, near misses and personal data breaches, what was done, and who was told and when, as the DfE cyber security standard and UK GDPR ask.

      • Live ICO 72-hour countdown
      • Report Fraud, DfE, RPA and governor reporting checks
      • Lessons learned, follow-up actions and an Excel export
      Open incident log →

      Clear all data

      Start again from scratch: removes everything this toolkit has saved in this browser. Export anything you want to keep to Excel first.

      Clear all saved data?

      This permanently deletes, from this browser only:

      • the DfE standards and Cyber Essentials trackers
      • the software, hardware, user accounts, backup and incident registers
      • the selected school or trust, and all files imported into the Trust Overview

      It cannot be undone. Export to Excel first if you want a copy, and close any other tabs with the toolkit open.
